## About This Game — [Overview]

ROI: Reign of Idlers brings the depth of a classic MMO to a game that respects your time. Recruit a team
of heroes and develop each one through classes, skills, and gear. Your party keeps fighting and growing
while you work, study, or step away — drop back in whenever you like to upgrade your team and plan your
next move.

Choose class paths, spend your stats, learn and upgrade skills, socket cards for lasting power, and shape
a team that is yours alone. Every session is short; the build behind it runs deep.

Assemble the line-up that fits your style, from a wall of archers to a storm of mages, and climb as far as
your build will take you.

## [Key Features]

- **Needs only a little of your time** — a few minutes to plan and upgrade, then your party carries on while you live your life.
- **Idle, but deep** — your party fights on its own; you own every decision behind it. Progress continues online, whether you are watching or away.
- **Deep character-building** — class paths, stat allocation, a growing skill set, and job advancement as you climb.
- **Gear that matters** — refine and enhance your equipment on the road to your strongest build.
- **Cards and sockets** — collect the essence of fallen monsters and slot them for lasting power.
- **Team-building** — mix classes into a line-up that fits your style, each with its own way to fight.
- **A living player market** — a shared market where gear and materials change hands between players, and what you find is worth something to someone.
- **One online world** — a single shared server and economy, your character verified and safe.
